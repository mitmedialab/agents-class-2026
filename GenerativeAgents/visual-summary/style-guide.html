4 · interaction and layout rules

from styles.css + AGENTS.md

states

  • press: move down 1px and drop the 1.5px shadow.
  • on / selected: aria-pressed or aria-selected fills with ink (or orange or green for heat toggles).
  • focus: 2px orange outline, 2px offset.
  • hover on faces and evidence pills: a thin ink or orange outline, no color fill.
  • live / in progress: orange border plus a 3px inset orange bar on the left.

shape and space

  • radius 6px for panels and cards, 999px for buttons and pills, 2–4px for squares and faces.
  • 1px --line rules; 1.5px ink borders only on controls.
  • gaps of 6, 8, 10, 12 and 14px; panel padding 10–14px.
  • pixel images use image-rendering:pixelated.

do / avoid

  • do: restrained, editorial, mostly monochrome; one accent at a time.
  • do: keyboard access, reduced motion, layouts that work on phone widths.
  • avoid: dashboard card grids, gradients, new fonts or new radii.
  • avoid: real names — agents appear by code and pixel portrait only.
