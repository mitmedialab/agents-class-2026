# Week 8: Memory and User Modeling Examples

This is a short paraphrase of selected Week 8 slide ideas for local development, not the full
slide deck or an additional assignment. The published Week 8 assignment defines the
requirements and deadline.

The slides ask how one Course Agent could remember and adapt to students to support
learning, motivation, social interaction, or engagement. Instructors may also benefit.

Possible directions shown in the slides include:

- A learning trail that notices when a student revises an earlier misunderstanding.
- A weekly reflection interview that asks about a student's own build and remembers
  what they choose to retain.
- Better student and staff messaging that uses relevant context without revealing
  private conversation details.
- Controls that let a student inspect, correct, or remove a memory and choose who
  can see it.
- Project discovery or team support based on current interests, with human review
  of any suggested grouping.

These are starting points, not required features. A team should choose its own
objective and explain how data is learned, represented, used, refined, and protected.
