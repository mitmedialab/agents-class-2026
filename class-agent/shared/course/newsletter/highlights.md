# How The Class Runtime chooses its highlights

**Newsletter:** The Class Runtime, the weekly newsletter of MAS.S60, Fall 2026
**Curated by:** The Course Agent
**Reviewed by:** The MAS.S60 teaching team
**Highlights per issue:** 4

Every week, the Course Agent reads what each student built and published, scores every build against the same rubric, and features the four that score highest. The teaching team reviews each issue before it is sent.

## What the Course Agent reads

- **Your repository, read-only.** The files in the week's folder, the notes you wrote there, and the commit messages from the week's build window. The Course Agent never writes to your repository.
- **Your post for the week.** The text of the post on your project site, and the pictures, diagrams, and videos in it.

A build is considered when there is something to look at for the week: files in the week's folder, commits during the week, or a page on your site named for the week.

## The rubric

Each build gets a score from 0 to 10 on four criteria, and the weighted total ranks the builds.

- **Originality, 30%.** How far outside the box is it? An unexpected idea, subject, or mechanism scores higher than the tutorial's default path, and ambition counts.
- **Assignment fit, 25%.** Did it do what this week's assignment asked, rather than something next to it?
- **Cognitive augmentation, 25%.** How directly does it help a person think, remember, learn, focus, or decide, while that person stays in charge?
- **Execution, 20%.** Does it work, and does the post show it with a demo, a trace, or an honest account of what failed?

A 5 is an ordinary complete submission, and 8 or more is exceptional. Only what the repository and the post show counts, so a claim without evidence earns nothing.

## How the four are picked

1. A build must score at least 5 on assignment fit. Builds below that rank behind every build that clears it.
2. Anyone featured in the last issue sits this one out, so the spotlight moves around the class.
3. The four highest weighted totals are featured. A tie goes to assignment fit, then to originality.

## What ends up in the issue

- **The picture** comes from your own post. The Course Agent looks at the images, diagrams, video stills, and canvases you published and picks the one that shows the build best. If none of them works, it uses a screenshot of the post.
- **The description** says in plain words what the build does and how it answers the assignment.
- **The closing quote** is usually a line from a student's post, copied word for word and checked against the source.
- **Every other build** with a post that week is listed at the end of the issue with a one-line description of what was built that week, linked to that week's post. A student with nothing made for the week is left off that week's list, even when their site holds earlier work.

## Who checks it

The Course Agent drafts every issue, and the teaching team reviews it before anything is sent. The scores exist only to choose the highlights, and only the teaching team sees them.

Questions about how the newsletter works can go to the Course Agent. Questions about a particular pick go to the teaching team.
