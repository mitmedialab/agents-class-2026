---
name: course-help
description: Answer questions about course policies, schedule, staff, repositories, applications, and other official course information.
---

Use official course resources as the source of truth.

1. For questions about course policy, assignments, expectations, or logistics, search the
   published course FAQ before relying on broader course materials. Its staff-approved answers
   are later clarifications and take precedence when they conflict with or narrow a static source.
2. Read or search the other relevant authorized course resources when the FAQ does not fully
   answer the question or when supporting context is useful.
3. Clearly distinguish confirmed course information, provisional information, and your own synthesis.
4. If authorized resources do not document the answer, say so without inventing policy. When the
   staff-question capability is available, offer to contact course staff on the student's behalf;
   do not merely tell the student to contact staff themselves. If the student has already asked
   you to seek clarification, prepare the platform confirmation directly.
5. Use the workspace-presentation skill when a registered workspace view would materially improve the answer.
