# Week 8 Build: Memory & User Modeling

Work with your predetermined Week 8 group to build a **personalization feature** on the [Course Agent](https://github.com/mitmedialab/agents-class-2026).

## 1. Define the objective

Choose a specific objective for your feature. Feel free to use the [Week 8 slides](https://cognitive-agents.media.mit.edu/?q=openweek8slides) for inspiration. Consider the following question:

> How can a course agent remember, adapt and personalize to the students (you!) to augment class learning outcomes, motivation, social interaction or engagement?

You may also design a feature that augments the instructors.

## 2. Characterize and build the agent

Fork the [Course Agent repository](https://github.com/mitmedialab/agents-class-2026), implement your feature, and open a **pull request** to the course repository.

Characterize the feature with a diagram showing the user, agent, memory or user model, and relevant tools and resources based on relevant specifications from the course.

If your feature is an agent memory architecture, please explain how the feature **encodes, stores, and retrieves** information, or if it is a personalization / user modeling feature, please explain how the agent **learns, represents, adapts, and refines** its user model. Show the feature working in a demo.

State whose information the feature remembers, where it came from, and who can access or change it.

Show one interaction in which information the agent learned about a user changes a later response or action, and explain how that change supports your objective.


## 3. Design an evaluation

In **1–2 paragraphs**, describe the most rigorous evaluation you would conduct if you could collect the data you need. What baseline or ablations would you compare your feature against? What data and metrics would show that it improved your chosen outcome for students or instructors? **Running the evaluation is optional.**

## 4. Document and submit

One group member may publish the full Week 8 writeup on their website. Every other member must link to it from their own website. In the writeup, include:

- Your objective and why personalization or advanced memory might help.
- Your agent diagram, observation space, and list of tools and actions.
- How the agent's memory or user model works.
- A link to your pull request and a demo or example interaction.
- Your 1–2 paragraph evaluation plan and, if you ran an evaluation, its results.
- A short reflection on what worked, what failed, and what your team would improve.
- Each member's contribution and a disclosure of AI assistance.

As in earlier weeks, write the documentation and reflection yourselves.

**Due Monday, November 9 at 11:59 PM ET.**

You may be randomly selected to present your work in class. Be ready to explain your objective, walk through the agent diagram, demonstrate the feature, and discuss what you learned.
